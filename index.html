How to play

  1. Pick a bet and a difficulty, press GO. The young poet leaps onto the first marble slab of the descent.
  2. Each slab may crumble into the lava — then the round is lost.
  3. Every safe slab raises the multiplier. Press CASH OUT any time after the first step.
  4. A Golden Idol can appear on a safe slab: it doubles your running multiplier (all upcoming slabs too).
  5. The run descends through Dante's nine circles, from Limbo to frozen Cocytus. Reaching the last slab or the max win of 10,000x cashes out automatically.

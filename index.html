Settings & Rules

How to play

  1. Pick a bet and a difficulty, press GO (or Space / Enter). The poet leaps onto the first marble slab.
  2. Each slab may crumble into the lava. Then the bet is lost.
  3. Every safe slab raises the multiplier. Press CASH OUT (or C) any time after the first safe step.
  4. A Golden Idol on a safe slab doubles the running multiplier, including every slab after it. Idols are inside the RTP.
  5. The run descends through Dante's nine circles, from Limbo to frozen Cocytus. The last slab, or the max win of 10,000x, cashes out on its own.

Stake mode

    Limits

    • Bet from 1 to 500 virtual coins. The demo starts at 1,000.
    • Max win is 10,000x the bet (5,000,000 coins at the max bet).
    • Multipliers are rounded down to 0.01x. The first safe step is always above 1.00x, so a cash-out is never shown as less than the stake.

    Return to player (operator setting)

    The selected RTP applies to every difficulty and to any cash-out strategy, up to rounding down. Outcomes are one integer per step from crypto.getRandomValues on this device: below the collapse line the slab falls, below the idol line it holds with a Golden Idol, otherwise it holds. Circles, animation and sound do not change the result.

    Unfinished round and faults

    • There is no decision timer. This demo restores an open round on the same device. A real-money build cashes an unfinished round out at the current multiplier after the operator's timeout (only once a safe step has landed); a round with no step yet is voided.
    • Malfunction voids all pays and plays.
    • Hotkeys: Space or Enter to step, C to cash out, Esc to close this panel.
